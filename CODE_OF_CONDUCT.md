# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This repository exists to help engineers across the organization share knowledge and build on each other's work. We are committed to keeping this a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free space for everyone who contributes, reviews, or otherwise participates, regardless of team, seniority, background, or personal identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and assume good intent.
- Credit the original authors and sources when adapting an existing Skill.
- Be patient with contributors who are new to the repository's conventions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks directed at any contributor.
- Dismissive or discriminatory comments related to background, identity, or experience level.
- Publishing others' private information, credentials, or confidential company data.
- Deliberately submitting misleading, unsafe, or malicious Skills or scripts.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the maintainers listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS) or through your usual internal channel for workplace concerns. Reports will be handled promptly and as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of behavior and may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict a contributor's participation for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
